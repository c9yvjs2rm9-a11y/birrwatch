We aggregate the published daily rate sheets of Ethiopia's commercial banks and NBE-licensed FX bureaus, and cross-check against the National Bank of Ethiopia's official reference rate. Observer accounts see end-of-day figures; Professional accounts see updates the moment a bank publishes.

If you're handing dollars to a bank and receiving birr, look at the bank's buy rate — higher is better for you. If you're paying birr to receive dollars, look at the sell rate — lower is better. The comparison table's toggle flips the highlight to the column you care about.
